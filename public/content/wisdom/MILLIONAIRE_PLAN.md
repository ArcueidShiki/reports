# The $10k → $1M Plan (5-Year Aggressive Track)

*Built June 2026. Not financial advice — this is a strategy framework with real math. Update quarterly.*

---

## The Core Truth

$10k invested in markets alone will NOT make you a millionaire in 5 years. Hitting $1M purely by investing requires **$13,400/month** in contributions at 8% returns. Nobody turns $10k into $1M trading — those who try usually lose the $10k.

The actual formula:

**Net worth = Investment portfolio + Business equity**

A business doing $20k/month profit is itself worth ~$700k (at a typical 3x annual profit multiple for online businesses) — AND it funds the portfolio. The business is the engine. Investing is the flywheel. You need both.

## The Three Scenarios (verified math, 8% market returns)

| | Year 1 invest/mo | Year 5 invest/mo | Portfolio @ 5yr | Business equity | **Net worth** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | $500 | $7,000 | $246k | ~$420k | **~$665k** |
| **Base** | $1,000 | $12,000 | $438k | ~$720k | **~$1.16M** ✓ |
| Aggressive | $1,500 | $20,000 | $717k | ~$1.2M | **~$1.9M** |

Even the conservative case makes you a millionaire by year ~7. The base case does it in 5. The variable that matters most is **how fast business income grows** — not stock picking.

---

## Phase 1: Cash Engine (Months 0–6) — Target: $3–5k/mo

Skip the "passive income" fantasy. The fastest path from $0 revenue is **selling a high-value service**. 2026 reality check from current data:

- AI-related freelance gigs grew **109% YoY** on Upwork; AI video editing +329%
- Specialized rates: AI/ML work $120–250/hr, prompt engineering $75–200/hr, AI workflow automation commands premium rates "because almost nobody knows how to do them well yet"
- Generic skills = commodity pricing. **Specialization = 4–8x income.**

**Action:**
1. Pick ONE skill at the intersection of (what you know) × (what pays): AI workflow automation, AI video/content production, web/app development with AI tooling, or technical consulting in your existing domain.
2. Spend ≤$500 of the $10k on tools/courses. Keep $9.5k as runway + emergency buffer.
3. Land first 3 clients in 60 days: direct outreach (not just platforms), document results publicly.
4. Raise rates every 2–3 clients. You should feel slightly uncomfortable quoting.

**Kill criterion:** If $0 revenue after 90 days of real effort, the niche or offer is wrong — change ONE variable and rerun.

## Phase 2: Productize (Months 6–18) — Target: $8–15k/mo

Hourly work caps out. Convert services into **productized offers with retainers**:

- AI automation agency model: $5k–50k per project, $500–5k/mo retainers, top solo operators at $20k+/mo
- Same deliverable, repeatable process, fixed price. Stop selling hours, start selling outcomes.
- Hire contractors for delivery at 30–50% of revenue once demand exceeds your hours.

**Investing kicks in here:** automate $1,000+/mo into index funds (VTI/VOO-style broad market). Don't pick stocks with money you'll need. Boring on purpose.

## Phase 3: Scale + Leverage (Months 18–60) — Target: $20–35k/mo

Layer scalable income on top of the service base:

- **Digital products** (70–90% margins): templates, courses, paid newsletter built from your client work. Warning from the data: >50% of creators earn <$15k/yr — products only work when you already have proof + audience from Phases 1–2.
- **Micro-SaaS** (optional, if technical): vertical tools for one industry's painful workflow. Median real business = $4.2k MRR; winners at $5–50k MRR. Avoid generic AI wrappers — 90% are failing, 60–70% earn zero.
- Newsletter benchmark: ~$1/subscriber/month across monetization methods.

**Portfolio at this stage:** $6–12k/mo into investments. 80% broad index funds, up to 20% higher-conviction positions if you must. Rebalance yearly. Max out tax-advantaged accounts first (this alone is worth tens of thousands).

## The 90-Day Sprint (start now)

| Weeks | Do |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Pick niche + skill. Define ONE specific offer ("I do X for Y clients, producing Z result"). Set up portfolio site + LinkedIn. |
| 3–4 | Build 2 proof pieces (free/discounted work for testimonials). Open brokerage + high-yield savings for the $9.5k. |
| 5–8 | Outreach: 10 personalized contacts/day. Goal: 3 paying clients. |
| 9–12 | Deliver exceptionally. Collect testimonials. Raise prices. Document everything (becomes your future content/products). |

## Rules (the part that actually determines the outcome)

1. **Never invest money you need within 3 years.** Business runway stays in cash/HYSA.
2. **No leverage, no options, no crypto moonshots** with core capital. Asymmetric bets only with money you can lose 100% of (<5% of net worth).
3. **Savings rate beats returns** at your stage. Going from investing $1k to $3k/mo matters more than going from 8% to 12% returns.
4. **Income concentration is the real risk.** One client >40% of revenue = priority #1 is diversifying.
5. **Track monthly:** revenue, profit, amount invested, net worth. What gets measured compounds.
