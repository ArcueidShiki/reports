The United States formally lifted its naval blockade of the Strait of Hormuz and Iranian ports after President Trump and Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian signed a 14-point memorandum of understanding to wind down the war, reopen the strait, and lift sanctions on Iranian oil immediately. WTI crude buckled to roughly $75 a barrel, its weakest since early March, and equities did the arithmetic in real time: cheaper energy is the one thing that can defuse the very inflation shock that forced Warsh hawkish. The dovish release valve the prior day's report had described in theory opened in practice.

The result was a rally with an unusually clear internal signature.
