2) ASMI AI — AI that handles real-world chores via actual phone calls
- PH: 461 upvotes / 142 comments (very high comment ratio) — launched ~Jun 13
- URL: https://www.producthunt.com/products/asmi-ai
- What: Personal AI with its own voice, personality, and dedicated phone number. Calls every morning at a chosen time; you talk through the day; it then calls services (dentist, salon, plumber, bank, insurance) or people to book/coordinate/resolve. Navigates IVR, waits on hold, handles complex conversations. Updates you via iMessage/WhatsApp. Acts only on explicit instruction.
- Business model: consumer subscription (assistant + phone number).
- Reviews (PH): "helped me call a specialist doctor I'd put off for 3 months"; team praised for caring whether the task actually got DONE, not just handled gracefully.
