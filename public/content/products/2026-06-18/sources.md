## 3. a16z — June 2026

- "Big Ideas 2026" series (Part 1 & 2): AI infra, apps, American Dynamism (defense, manufacturing, aerospace, industrial), crypto.
- Core thesis: software alone no longer enough; capital wants software attached to "hard consequences," real infrastructure, regulated systems, or strong network effects.
- a16z Speedrun (Andrew Lee): **"Fat startups win in 2026."** Successful AI companies bundle software + data + hardware + human operations into full-stack solutions; outperform lean startups in slow-moving industries.
  - "A fat startup ships outcomes, not features." Winning model: **"AI does the work, you guarantee the outcome, the customer pays for the result."**
  - "Done-for-you" AI services: AI bookkeeping (not bookkeeping software), AI tax prep (not tax software), AI website builds (not a website builder); humans do QC, AI does scale.
  - Outcome-based pricing aligns vendor/user incentives; captures the gap between the cost of a human and the cost to complete the task with AI.
- Market context: Anthropic #1 most valuable private AI co. after ~$65B Series H, run-rate revenue ~$47B (May 2026). Waymo $16B round (Feb 2026) at $126B post; 15M paid trips in 2025. Cursor/Anysphere 150K+ paying devs, ~$2B revenue run-rate.
