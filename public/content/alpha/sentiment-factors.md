# Sentiment & Qualitative Factors

> Format: Factor Name | Signal | Condition | Applicable Types | Source

---

## Insider Activity

### Insider Buying / Share Buybacks
- **Signal**: Corporate insiders purchasing shares or company executing buyback program
- **Condition**: Recent insider buys confirmed via SEC filings (Form 4); buyback program actively reducing share count
- **Applicable**: All equities; especially distressed / turnaround candidates
- **Tags**: `insider` `sentiment` `contrarian`
- **Source**: Michael Lauer · *Stock Market Wizards*

---

## Catalyst / Event-Driven

### Price Catalyst
- **Signal**: Identifiable upcoming event expected to unlock value (restructuring, spin-off, turnaround plan, management change)
- **Condition**: Concrete plan announced or credible catalyst visible within 12–18 months
- **Applicable**: Distressed stocks, turnaround candidates
- **Tags**: `catalyst` `event-driven` `turnaround`
- **Source**: Michael Lauer · *Stock Market Wizards*

---

## Narrative / Story Quality

### Story/Narrative Appeal (Malkiel)
- **Signal**: Stock possesses an imaginative theme with investor appeal; a compelling story that can attract institutional attention
- **Condition**: Qualitative assessment; must be paired with at least one hard financial filter
- **Applicable**: Concept-driven, growth-narrative stocks
- **Tags**: `narrative` `sentiment` `growth`
- **Source**: Burton G. Malkiel · *A Random Walk Down Wall Street*

---

## Industry Knowledge (Qualitative Gate)

### Circle of Competence Check
- **Signal**: Investor has genuine operational or industry expertise in the company's domain
- **Condition**: Cannot invest unless you can describe the business model, competitive moat, and key risk factors without looking them up
- **Applicable**: All equities (especially concentrated positions)
- **Tags**: `qualitative` `discipline` `concentration`
- **Source**: Michael Lauer · *Stock Market Wizards*; Warren Buffett · Berkshire Letters
