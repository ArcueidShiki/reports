# Value Factors (Fundamental)

> Format: Factor Name | Formula | Condition | Applicable Types | Source

## Profitability

### ROE (Return on Equity)
- **Formula**: Net Profit / Shareholder Equity
- **Condition**: > 20% for past 10 years; no single year < 15%
- **Applicable**: All equities; strongest signal for moat businesses
- **Tags**: `quality` `profitability` `long-term`
- **Source**: Warren Buffett · Berkshire Letters; David Polen · Polen Capital

### ROA (Return on Assets)
- **Formula**: Net Profit / Total Assets
- **Condition**: > 10%, sustained 3–5 years
- **Applicable**: Asset-intensive companies, small-cap
- **Tags**: `quality` `profitability`
- **Source**: W. Whitney George · Royce Funds

### Return on Capital (ROC)
- **Formula**: EBIT / (Net Operating Assets + Net Fixed Assets)
- **Condition**: > 25% (ROA proxy); above market average sustained 3–5 years
- **Applicable**: All equities; capital-intensive sectors
- **Tags**: `quality` `greenblatt`
- **Source**: Joel Greenblatt · *The Little Book That Beats the Market*

### Gross Margin
- **Formula**: Gross Profit / Operating Revenue
- **Condition**: > 40%
- **Applicable**: All equities; strongest for consumer brands, software
- **Tags**: `quality` `moat`
- **Source**: Warren Buffett · Berkshire Letters

## Valuation

### P/E Ratio
- **Formula**: Stock Price / Earnings Per Share (or Market Cap / Net Income)
- **Condition**: < 20 (general); < 40% of 5-year peak P/E; < 70th historical percentile
- **Applicable**: Profitable companies; not suitable for loss-making or early-stage
- **Tags**: `valuation` `screener`
- **Source**: Benjamin Graham · *The Intelligent Investor*; W. Whitney George · Royce Funds

### Earnings Yield (Graham)
- **Formula**: 1 / P/E = Net Profit / Total Market Value
- **Condition**: > 2× AAA Bond Yield (reference: ~9.74% in Graham era; adjust to current)
- **Applicable**: Stable earnings firms
- **Tags**: `valuation` `graham` `bond-relative`
- **Source**: Benjamin Graham · *Security Analysis*

### Earnings Yield (Greenblatt)
- **Formula**: EBIT / (Total Shareholder Equity Market Value + Net Interest-Bearing Debt)
- **Condition**: Rank top quartile within universe; P/E ≤ 20 threshold
- **Applicable**: All equities > $50M market cap; excludes utilities, financials
- **Tags**: `valuation` `greenblatt` `ev-based`
- **Source**: Joel Greenblatt · *The Little Book That Beats the Market*

### P/B Ratio (Price-to-Book)
- **Formula**: Market Cap / Net Assets (Book Value)
- **Condition**: < 2/3 per-share tangible assets (Graham strict); < 2 (Berry); ≤ 1.0 (Lauer distressed); < 1 for small-cap
- **Applicable**: Asset-rich companies; cyclicals; distressed situations
- **Tags**: `valuation` `asset-based` `cyclical` `small-cap`
- **Source**: Benjamin Graham · *Security Analysis*; Michael A. Berry; Michael Lauer; W. Whitney George

### P/S Ratio (Price-to-Sales)
- **Formula**: Stock Price / Sales Per Share
- **Condition**: Lowest 10% within universe outperforms
- **Applicable**: Growth stocks, loss-making firms, early-stage
- **Tags**: `valuation` `growth` `loss-making`
- **Source**: James O'Shaughnessy · *What Works on Wall Street*

### PEG Ratio (Lynch Adjusted)
- **Formula**: P/E / (LT Earnings Growth Rate + Dividend Yield) — Lynch calls ≥ 2 attractive
- **Condition**: (LT Earnings Growth Rate + Dividend Yield) / P/E ≥ 2; PEG ≤ 1 (Malkiel)
- **Applicable**: All equities; most meaningful for fast-growth stocks
- **Tags**: `valuation` `growth-adjusted` `lynch`
- **Source**: Peter Lynch · *One Up on Wall Street*; Burton Malkiel · *A Random Walk Down Wall Street*

### Forward P/E (Berry 2-2-2)
- **Formula**: Current Price / Estimated Forward Earnings
- **Condition**: Forward P/E < Market Average P/E × 0.5
- **Applicable**: Stable earners; cyclicals
- **Tags**: `valuation` `forward-looking` `contrarian`
- **Source**: Michael A. Berry & David Dreman · *Financial Analysts Journal*

### EBITDA/EV
- **Formula**: EBITDA / Enterprise Value
- **Condition**: Highest ratio in universe performs best
- **Applicable**: All equities; particularly capital-intensive
- **Tags**: `valuation` `ev-based` `oshaughnessy`
- **Source**: James O'Shaughnessy · *What Works on Wall Street*

### Price-to-Cash Flow
- **Formula**: Stock Price / Cash Flow Per Share
- **Condition**: Lower preferred; rank bottom quartile of universe
- **Applicable**: All equities
- **Tags**: `valuation` `cash-flow`
- **Source**: James O'Shaughnessy · *What Works on Wall Street*

### Asset Value Discount (Graham)
- **Formula**: Stock Price vs. Per-Share Tangible Assets
- **Condition**: Stock Price < 2/3 × Per-Share Tangible Assets
- **Applicable**: Asset-rich, traditional industries
- **Tags**: `valuation` `deep-value` `graham`
- **Source**: Benjamin Graham · *Security Analysis*

### Working Capital Discount (Graham)
- **Formula**: Stock Price vs. Per-Share Net Working Capital
- **Condition**: Stock Price < 2/3 × Per-Share Net Working Capital (Net-Net)
- **Applicable**: Extremely conservative selection; often micro-cap
- **Tags**: `valuation` `net-net` `graham` `deep-value`
- **Source**: Benjamin Graham · *Security Analysis*

## Balance Sheet / Solvency

### Debt-to-Equity Ratio
- **Formula**: Total Liabilities / Shareholder Equity
- **Condition**: < 1.5× (Buffett); Long-term debt / equity ≤ 1/3 (Lynch)
- **Applicable**: All equities
- **Tags**: `solvency` `leverage`
- **Source**: Warren Buffett · Berkshire Letters; Peter Lynch · *One Up on Wall Street*

### Debt-to-Assets Ratio
- **Formula**: Total Debt / Total Assets
- **Condition**: ≤ 25% (Lynch); < 50% (Polen, George); Total Debt < Tangible Assets (Graham)
- **Applicable**: All equities
- **Tags**: `solvency` `leverage`
- **Source**: Peter Lynch; David Polen; W. Whitney George; Benjamin Graham

### Current Ratio
- **Formula**: Current Assets / Current Liabilities
- **Condition**: > 2.0
- **Applicable**: Solvent companies; manufacturing, retail
- **Tags**: `liquidity` `graham`
- **Source**: Benjamin Graham · *Security Analysis*

### Quick Ratio
- **Formula**: (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities
- **Condition**: > 2.0
- **Applicable**: Any company; distressed situations especially
- **Tags**: `liquidity` `distressed`
- **Source**: Michael Lauer · *Stock Market Wizards*

### Debt-to-Working Capital
- **Formula**: Total Debt / Net Working Capital
- **Condition**: < 2× Net Working Capital
- **Applicable**: Short-term stability check
- **Tags**: `solvency` `graham`
- **Source**: Benjamin Graham · *Security Analysis*

## Cash Flow

### Free Cash Flow (FCF)
- **Formula**: Operating Cash Flow − Capital Expenditures
- **Condition**: > 0; above market average
- **Applicable**: All equities; critical for value & quality assessment
- **Tags**: `cash-flow` `quality`
- **Source**: Warren Buffett; Peter Lynch; David Polen

### Net Cash Per Share
- **Formula**: (Total Cash − Total Liabilities) / Share Count
- **Condition**: > 0
- **Applicable**: All equities; especially value/turnaround
- **Tags**: `cash-flow` `lynch`
- **Source**: Peter Lynch · *One Up on Wall Street*

## Growth

### Earnings Growth Rate
- **Formula**: Net Income YoY % change; 10-year average annual growth
- **Condition**: ≥ 20% annual (fast-growth); > 7% annual (minimum for growth-oriented); > market average for ≥ 5 consecutive years (Malkiel)
- **Applicable**: Fast-growth: ≥ 20%; stable-growth: ≥ 7%; slow-growth: GDP-linked
- **Tags**: `growth` `quality`
- **Source**: Peter Lynch; James O'Shaughnessy; Burton Malkiel; Benjamin Graham

### Earnings Consistency
- **Formula**: Count of years with net profit growth < −5% over 10-year window
- **Condition**: Maximum 2 occurrences in 10 years
- **Applicable**: Stable, mature companies
- **Tags**: `quality` `consistency` `graham`
- **Source**: Benjamin Graham · *Security Analysis*

### Inventory Growth vs. Revenue Growth
- **Formula**: Inventory YoY growth rate vs. Revenue YoY growth rate
- **Condition**: Inventory Growth Rate < Revenue Growth Rate (if inverse, red flag)
- **Applicable**: Cyclical companies (auto, steel, chemicals, airline)
- **Tags**: `cyclical` `inventory` `lynch`
- **Source**: Peter Lynch · *Beating the Street*

## Shareholder Returns

### Dividend Yield
- **Formula**: Annual Dividend / Stock Price
- **Condition**: > 2/3 × AAA Bond Yield (Graham reference: ~3.25%); sustainability through recessions
- **Applicable**: Dividend payers; slow-growth stocks
- **Tags**: `income` `dividend` `graham`
- **Source**: Benjamin Graham; Peter Lynch

### Buyback Yield
- **Formula**: (Prior Year Shares − Current Year Shares) / Prior Year Shares
- **Condition**: Higher ratio significantly outperforms; a strong standalone factor
- **Applicable**: All equities with share repurchase programs
- **Tags**: `shareholder-return` `buyback` `oshaughnessy`
- **Source**: James O'Shaughnessy · *What Works on Wall Street*

### Shareholder Yield
- **Formula**: Dividend Yield + Buyback Yield
- **Condition**: Outperforms either component in isolation
- **Applicable**: All equities
- **Tags**: `shareholder-return` `combined` `oshaughnessy`
- **Source**: James O'Shaughnessy · *What Works on Wall Street*

## Multi-Factor Models

### Greenblatt Magic Formula Rank
- **Components**: ROC rank + Earnings Yield rank (both ranked independently, summed)
- **Selection**: Lowest combined rank score = highest quality-value combination
- **Universe**: > $50M market cap; excludes utilities, financials, foreign ADRs, P/E < 5
- **Portfolio**: 20–30 stocks; 5–7 added every 2–3 months; annual reset
- **Tags**: `multi-factor` `greenblatt` `systematic`
- **Source**: Joel Greenblatt · *The Little Book That Beats the Market*

### O'Shaughnessy Trend-Value Model (Best of 300+ strategies)
- **Components**: Market cap > $200M + Top 10% multi-factor value score (P/B, P/E, P/S, EBITDA/EV, P/CF, Shareholder Yield) + Best 6-month price appreciation
- **Selection**: Top 25–50 stocks by composite score
- **Tags**: `multi-factor` `trend` `momentum` `oshaughnessy`
- **Source**: James O'Shaughnessy · *What Works on Wall Street*

### Berry 2-2-2 Rule
- **Components**: Forward P/E < Market Average × 0.5 AND Expected EPS Growth > Industry Average × 0.5 AND P/B < 2
- **Applicable**: Stable earners; cyclicals (oil & gas, mining)
- **Tags**: `multi-factor` `contrarian` `berry`
- **Source**: Berry & Dreman · *Financial Analysts Journal*

### Lynch 6-Type Framework
- **Types**: Slow-Growth · Stable-Growth · Fast-Growth · Cyclical · Turnaround · Asset-Rich
- **Logic**: Select dominant factor per type rather than one-size-fits-all screening
- **Tags**: `classification` `lynch` `multi-factor`
- **Source**: Peter Lynch · *One Up on Wall Street*

### Pre-Tax Profit Margin
- **Formula**: Pre-Tax Income / Revenue
- **Condition**: Industry-relative; Lynch uses it to compare within sector — higher than peers indicates pricing power or operational efficiency
- **Applicable**: All equities; most useful for cross-sector comparison
- **Tags**: `quality` `profitability` `lynch`
- **Source**: Peter Lynch · *One Up on Wall Street*; *Beating the Street*
